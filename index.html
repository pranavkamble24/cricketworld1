About ICC
he BCCI (India), Cricket Australia (CA), and ECB (England) are the powerful governing bodies for cricket in their nations, forming the "Big Three" that wield significant financial and governance influence in world cricket via the ICC, controlling revenue and major decisions, while constantly discussing ways to enhance top-tier Test cricket between themselves, like a potential two-tier system. BCCI is the world's richest board, CA leads in overall ICC titles, and the ECB manages England Wales cricket.
