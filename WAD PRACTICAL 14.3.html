England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB)



England's cricket team, governed by the England and Wales Cricket Board, competes in all three international formats: Test, One Day International (ODI), and Twenty20 International (T20I). Known for their aggressive "Bazball" style in Test cricket and white-ball dominance in recent years, they are the current holders of the T20 World Cup title, winning it in 2022 [1, 2]. England has notably secured the ODI World Cup once, at home in 2019, and the T20 World Cup twice [3, 4].
